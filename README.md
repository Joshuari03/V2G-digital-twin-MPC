**V2G Digital Twin with MPC**

A digital twin of a Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) system, integrated with Model Predictive Control (MPC) to manage bidirectional power flow between electric vehicles and the grid. The model simulates the system's behaviour, while the controller optimises charging and discharging over a prediction horizon, subject to battery and grid constraints.
